## What happened next

Dig's finite-horizon pairwise separation

```text
D_C,T^2(i,j) = integral_0^T ||h_i(t)-h_j(t)||^2 dt
```

was stripped of the neuron-specific story and reused in TWC as conditional measurement information:

```text
I_c(S) = min_beta ||g_S - J_S beta||^2.
```

There the alternatives are hidden physical parameter directions rather than dendritic source locations.

The object immediately became useful because TWC already had two exact response-equivalent topology gauges and known physical perturbations predicted to break them.

The new capability calculation correctly localized essentially all gauge-breaking information to those independently predicted perturbations, and the prescription remained correct in all 15 original noisy wrong-topology fitted points.

Thus the useful cross-repo lesson is not `neurons are Clockfields`.

It is:

```text
receiver / measurement operator
+ alternative causes
+ nuisance directions
+ finite observation budget
=
explicit information geometry and experiment-design choices.
```

## Why this matters to future GeometricNeuron work

If this repo resumes biological address/morphology experiments, do not measure geometry because it looks interesting.

Specify first:

```text
candidate alternatives
receiver/readout C
observation/task horizon
noise / nuisance directions
```

Then ask which morphology/mechanism changes **conditional distinguishability relevant to the task**.

This is harder to fool with pretty modes and much closer to an engineering or biological question.
